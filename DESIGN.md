## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intimacy, precision, and timeless elegance of high-fashion bridal couture. Designed for discerning couples and elite event patrons, the aesthetic balances the literary stillness of Kinfolk with the grand architectural romance of high-end editorial publications like Vogue Weddings and Harper's Bazaar Bride.

The visual style relies on **Disciplined Editorial Minimalism**:
- Generous, deliberate white space acting as a physical pause.
- Disciplined layout geometry paired with delicate, high-contrast typography.
- Restraint over excess: tactile warmth replaces digital synthetic surfaces; fine hairline strokes replace heavy shadows.
- An emotional tone that feels bespoke, cinematic, quietly confident, and exquisitely organized.

## Colors

The palette avoids stark pure whites and deep artificial blacks, anchoring itself in organic linen, stone, and warm pigments.

- **Primary (`#1F1E1D`)**: Warm charcoal ink. Used for headlines, high-emphasis text, structural keylines, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Champagne gold. Applied selectively to interactive accents, active tabs, subtle iconography, and refined badges.
- **Tertiary (`#E8DED8`)**: Soft muted blush-rose. Used for subtle highlights, romantic accents, selection states, and delicate callouts.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5`)**: Warm bridal ivory base canvas. Layered with secondary stone tints (`#F5F1EB`, `#EAE4DC`) to create structural panels without harsh division.
- **Text & Tone Structure**:
  - Primary text: Warm charcoal `#1F1E1D`
  - Secondary/Caption text: Espresso wash `#66605B`
  - Hairline borders: Neutral stone tint `#DFD7CC` (or `rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.12)`)

## Typography

The typographical pairing evokes classic print monographs:
- **Playfair Display**: Romantic, high-contrast serif with sculpted serifs. Reserved for displays, page headers, quote pulls, and event chapter titles. Italic variants are actively used for emotional punctuation, French phrases, and editorial accents.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Light-footed, modern grotesque sans-serif with subtle geometric warmth. Chosen over clinical sans-serifs to preserve softness and human warmth across logistical wedding timelines, budgets, and administrative dashboards.
- **Labels and Eyebrows**: Set exclusively in uppercase with tracking between `0.14em` and `0.18em` to mirror luxury atelier labeling and high-fashion spreads.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column editorial grid for desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column flow with offset visual blocks on mobile:
- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, `3rem` (48px) gutters, and minimum `6rem` (96px) margins. Max content container is capped at 1440px to preserve comfortable reading line lengths and magazine-like aspect ratios.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, `2rem` (32px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `1.5rem` (24px) margins.

Spacing follows an unhurried, breathable cadence. Section boundaries utilize expansive gaps (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) to allow imagery and text to command solitary attention. Asymmetry is encouraged: photo compositions may span 7 columns while accompanying narrative details span 4 columns with a 1-column negative space offset.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects simulated drop shadows in favor of **Low-Contrast Hairlines & Tonal Framing**:
- **Borders & Rules**: 1px fine hairline borders (`#DFD7CC`) define structural containers, table dividers, and imagery surrounds.
- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved by placing pale stone-tinted containers (`#F5F1EB` or `#EAE4DC`) over the ivory base (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Floating Modals & Overlays**: When elevation is mandatory (such as venue tour popovers or registry drawers), use an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow tinted with warm charcoal:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -12px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.03);`
- **Backdrops**: Modals and full-screen overlays feature a warm frosted treatment using `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)` with a gentle blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The design system strictly adheres to **Sharp (`0px`)** geometry. 

- All buttons, input boxes, cards, image containers, dialogs, and tags employ crisp 90-degree corners.
- This architectural precision reinforces the high-end stationery and printed publication reference, evoking thick matte paper cards, invitations, and framed photography.
- Subtle contrast to the rigid rectilinear geometry is introduced purely through rounded typography strokes and occasional pill-shaped botanical or circular seal imagery elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid warm charcoal (`#1F1E1D`) background, ivory (`#FAF8F5`) uppercase text, zero border-radius. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: subtly fades to `#2B2927` with a fine gold hairline outline or smooth opacity transition (`opacity: 0.9`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent background with a crisp 1px hairline border (`#1F1E1D` or `#C5A880`). Padding: `13px 27px`. Text in charcoal or champagne gold.
- **Tertiary / Text Link**: Unbordered, uppercase with wide tracking (`0.14em`). Features an offset animated 1px underline that expands on hover.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Single-line bottom border or 1px hairline perimeter in `#DFD7CC`.
- Background is transparent or faint stone (`#F5F1EB`).
- Focus state shifts the border color to champagne gold (`#C5A880`) without thick focus rings.
- Floating labels in `label-md` uppercase typography.

### Cards & Moodboard Tiles
- Background in natural ivory (`#FAF8F5`) or warm stone (`#F5F1EB`) surrounded by a 1px border (`#DFD7CC`).
- Photography inside cards is strictly rectangular with no corner rounding, presented with generous internal matting (16px to 24px) mirroring framed gallery prints.
- On hover, cards avoid elevation jumps; instead, subtle image zooms (scale 1.02) and delicate border color changes to `#C5A880` indicate interactivity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: Sharp 16px square with a 1px charcoal border. Checked state fills with `#1F1E1D` and displays an ivory checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Sharp diamond or square nested marker, or classic circular 16px border with an inner charcoal dot to maintain editorial crispness.

### Chips & Badges
- Low-profile rectangular badges with `label-sm` tracking.
- Bordered in `#DFD7CC` with soft stone (`#EAE4DC`) or muted rose (`#E8DED8`) fills.

### Lists & Itinerary Timelines
- Step-by-step wedding day timelines rely on vertical 1px hairline rules with centered numerical marks in Playfair Display italic.
- Items are separated by generous spacing (`space-lg`) rather than enclosed boxes, emphasizing effortless sequential narrative.
